Primary outcomes (per arm; the only outcomes that drive the decision)
  P1  rank x lr interaction on validation macro-F1, all ranks x all lrs.
      Seed-blocked, heteroscedasticity-robust Wald test of the 16 interaction
      contrasts; wild bootstrap under the additive model with seed effects
      held fixed, B = 9,999, alpha 0.05.
      Sensitivity (reported): whole-seed cluster bootstrap (Webb weights).
  P2  tuned spread <= 0.5 x median fixed-lr spread (test accuracy; the
      Study 3 rule unchanged). The continuous ratio is reported with a 95%
      interval from a whole-seed bootstrap.
  An arm REPLICATES if P1 and P2 both hold.
  All three replicate -> stop expanding the main experiments, write the paper.
  Otherwise -> discuss with the supervisor; no new arms.

Secondary confirmatory (per arm; reported, not decision-relevant)
  C1  r=16 > r=1 at lr 3e-4  AND  r=1 > r=16 at lr 3e-3
  C2  r=8  > r=2 at lr 3e-4  AND  r=2 > r=8  at lr 3e-3
  One-sided paired t (paired by seed) per direction; contrast p = max of the

Disclosures
  1. The classical ANOVA F-test used in Study 3 is anti-conservative here
     (18% false positives under a true null) because cell variances differ
     greatly. Applied post hoc to Study 3 (3 seeds), the v2 test gives
     all ranks p = 0.006 (conclusion unchanged); middle ranks {2,4,8}
     p = 0.04 (previously 0.002).
  2. P2 is demanding: it passes with high probability only if the effect is
     close to the Study 3 size (about 0.1 at half size). The continuous ratio
     and its interval are therefore always reported.
  3. S1 power with 5 seeds at the Study 3 effect size: about 0.6-0.8 per contrast.
  4. If the 1.5B model prefers lower learning rates overall, the fixed S1
     learning rates (3e-4, 3e-3) may not straddle its optimum.
